Biography

Dr. Sarah Johnson is a highly experienced cardiologist with over 15 years of practice. She completed her medical degree at Addis Ababa University and specialized in interventional cardiology at Johns Hopkins Hospital in the United States.

She is known for her expertise in complex cardiac procedures and her compassionate approach to patient care. Dr. Johnson has published numerous research papers in leading medical journals and is actively involved in medical education.
