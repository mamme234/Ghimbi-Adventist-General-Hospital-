Antenatal Care Program

Our comprehensive antenatal program ensures you receive the best care throughout your pregnancy journey.

1-3 Months
Initial assessment, blood tests, dating scan, and nutritional counseling
4-6 Months
Regular check-ups, anomaly scan, glucose testing, and fetal monitoring
7-9 Months
Weekly visits, growth scans, birth plan preparation, and delivery planning
