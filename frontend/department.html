About the Department

The Cardiology Department at Ghimbi Adventist General Hospital is a center of excellence dedicated to the diagnosis, treatment, and prevention of heart and cardiovascular diseases. Our team of internationally trained cardiologists uses cutting-edge technology to provide comprehensive cardiac care.

From routine check-ups to complex surgical procedures, we offer a full spectrum of cardiac services. Our state-of-the-art catheterization lab, advanced imaging capabilities, and dedicated cardiac intensive care unit ensure the highest quality of care.
