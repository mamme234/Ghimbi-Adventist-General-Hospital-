First Aid

First Aid Tips

Basic first aid knowledge can save lives

CPR

If someone is unconscious and not breathing, call 911 and start CPR immediately.

Bleeding Control

Apply direct pressure to the wound with a clean cloth. Maintain pressure until help arrives.

Burns

Cool the burn with running water for at least 20 minutes. Cover with a clean cloth.

Fractures

Immobilize the injured area. Use a splint if available and seek medical help.

Choking

Perform the Heimlich maneuver. Call 911 if the person cannot breathe.

Heat Stroke

Move to a cool area, remove excess clothing, and apply cool water to the skin.
