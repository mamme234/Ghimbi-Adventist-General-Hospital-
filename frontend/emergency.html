Safety First

Emergency Preparedness Tips

Be prepared for emergencies with these important guidelines

01

Stay Calm

Panic can worsen the situation. Take a deep breath and assess the situation carefully.

02

Call Emergency

Dial our emergency number immediately. Provide clear information about the situation.

03

Provide Information

Be ready to share the patient's condition, location, and any relevant medical history.

04

Follow Instructions

Listen carefully to emergency dispatchers and follow their guidance until help arrives.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about emergency services

What should I do in a medical emergency?

Call our emergency hotline immediately at +251 911 234 567. Stay calm, provide clear information, and follow instructions from our emergency team.

Is the emergency department open 24/7?

Yes, our emergency department operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including all holidays.

Do you have ambulance services?

Yes, we have a fleet of advanced life support ambulances available 24/7 for emergency response.
