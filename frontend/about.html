Our History

A Legacy of Healthcare Excellence

Founded in 2010, MediCare Hospital has grown from a small clinic to one of Ethiopia's leading healthcare institutions. Our journey is marked by continuous innovation, compassionate care, and unwavering commitment to patient well-being.

Over the past decade, we have expanded our services, invested in cutting-edge technology, and assembled a team of world-class medical professionals. Today, we stand as a beacon of hope for thousands of patients across the country.
