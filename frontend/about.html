Our Story

Excellence in Healthcare Since 2027

Gimbi Adventist General Hospital is a premier healthcare institution dedicated to providing compassionate, high-quality medical care to the community. Founded in 2027, we have grown to become a trusted name in healthcare, combining advanced medical technology with the healing touch of compassionate care.

Our hospital is built on the principles of excellence, integrity, and service. We believe that every patient deserves the best possible care, and we work tirelessly to ensure that our patients receive treatment that is both effective and compassionate.

Our Core

Mission, Vision & Values

Guiding principles that shape our commitment to healthcare excellence

Our Mission

To provide compassionate, high-quality healthcare services that improve the health and well-being of our community, guided by the principles of excellence, integrity, and service.

Our Vision

To be the leading healthcare institution in the region, recognized for excellence in patient care, medical innovation, and community service, setting the standard for healthcare delivery.

Our Values

Compassion: Treating every patient with dignity and care.
Excellence: Striving for the highest standards in everything we do.
Integrity: Being honest, transparent, and ethical.
Innovation: Embracing new technologies and approaches.
Service: Putting patients and community first.
