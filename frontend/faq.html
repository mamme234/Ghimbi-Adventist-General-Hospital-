Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our hospital and services

  3. FAQ

What are the hospital visiting hours?

General visiting hours are from 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM daily. ICU visiting hours are from 4:00 PM to 6:00 PM. Please check with the department for any specific restrictions or special visiting arrangements.

How do I get to MediCare Hospital?

We are located in Addis Ababa, Bole Sub-city, Woreda 03, near Bole International Airport. You can use public transportation or taxi services. We have ample parking available for visitors and patients.

What documents should I bring for my visit?

Please bring your ID, insurance card, referral letter (if any), previous medical records, and list of current medications. If you're a new patient, arrive 15 minutes early for registration.

How do I book an appointment?

You can book an appointment through our online booking system at appointments.html, call us at +251 911 234 567, or visit our reception desk in person. We recommend booking at least 2-3 days in advance.

Can I cancel or reschedule my appointment?

Yes, you can cancel or reschedule your appointment by calling our reception or through the patient portal. Please provide at least 24 hours notice to avoid cancellation fees.

What if I need a same-day appointment?

We reserve a limited number of same-day appointments for urgent cases. Please call us early in the morning to check availability or visit our emergency department for urgent medical needs.

What insurance plans do you accept?

We accept most major insurance plans. Please contact our billing department at +251 911 234 567 ext. 404 to confirm if your specific insurance is accepted.

How do I pay my hospital bill?

You can pay your bill online through the patient portal, at our billing office, or via bank transfer. We accept cash, credit/debit cards, and mobile money payments.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes, we offer flexible payment plans for patients who need financial assistance. Please speak with our billing department to discuss your options.

What diagnostic services are available?

We offer comprehensive diagnostic services including laboratory testing, radiology (X-Ray, MRI, CT Scan, Ultrasound), cardiac testing, and specialized pathology services.

How long does it take to get test results?

Most test results are available within 24-48 hours. Emergency results are available within a few hours. You can access your results through the patient portal.

What should I do in a medical emergency?

In a medical emergency, call 911 immediately. If you're at or near the hospital, go directly to our emergency department. Do not wait if you experience chest pain, difficulty breathing, severe bleeding, or sudden confusion.

Is the emergency department open 24/7?

Yes, our emergency department is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year. We have a dedicated team of emergency physicians and nurses always ready to provide immediate care.

Does MediCare have ambulance services?

Yes, we have a fleet of fully equipped ambulances staffed by trained paramedics. Call our emergency number at +251 911 234 567 to request an ambulance.
